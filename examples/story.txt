Scene 1: The Missed Train

Maya reached the platform just as the last train doors slid shut.
She stood there, breathless, watching the red tail lights disappear into the tunnel.
An old man on the bench smiled and said, "Sometimes missing one train gets you on the right one."
Maya almost laughed, but instead she sat down beside him and waited.

Scene 2: The Right One

Twenty minutes later, the next train arrived nearly empty.
Maya found a seat, looked up, and froze - across from her sat Arjun, her childhood friend she had not seen in ten years.
They stared for one second, then both smiled like no time had passed.
By the time their stop came, Maya knew the old man had been right.
