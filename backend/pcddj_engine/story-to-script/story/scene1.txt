🎧 Shadowless — First Meeting Scene

Early evening.

A public park rests between daylight and night.
Distant traffic hums. Children’s voices fade away one by one.
Leaves rustle softly.

A single bench sits under a half-lit streetlamp.

Ayaan approaches.
His footsteps slow. He sits.

The bench gives a tired creak.

Silence.

He exhales—long, practiced, like someone letting the day fall off his shoulders.

AYAAN
(murmuring, almost to himself)
Still my favorite place to disappear.

A pause.

Then—

SAANVI (O.S.)
You disappear a lot for someone who wants to be noticed.

Ayaan freezes.

No footsteps.
No shift on the bench.

AYAAN
(startled)
Sorry—
I didn’t see you there.

A beat.

SAANVI
That’s not surprising.

Ayaan turns his head.

She’s sitting beside him.

Too close.
Too still.

AYAAN
(confused, polite)
Uh… do you come here often?

SAANVI
(smiling, but distant)
Every day.
Just… not always like this.

The wind moves through the trees.
For a moment, it sounds almost like a whisper.

Ayaan studies her, uneasy.

AYAAN
You scared me.

SAANVI
I didn’t mean to.
People usually don’t notice me enough for that.

A pause.

Crickets begin—soft, uneven.

AYAAN
(half-laugh)
Guess we’re both good at being invisible then.

She tilts her head slightly.

SAANVI
No.
You choose it.

That lands heavier than expected.

AYAAN
…What?

She looks ahead, not at him.

SAANVI
You sit here every evening.
Same bench. Same time.
Waiting for the day to feel different.

Silence.

The traffic fades a little—like the world stepping back.

AYAAN
(low, careful)
You’ve been watching me?

SAANVI
Only because you looked lonely in a way that doesn’t ask for help.

Ayaan swallows.

AYAAN
What’s your name?

A pause. Just long enough to feel intentional.

SAANVI
Saanvi.

He nods.

AYAAN
I’m—

SAANVI
Ayaan.

Beat.

The bench creaks again as he shifts.

AYAAN
(smiles, uneasy)
Okay… now that was creepy.

She finally looks at him.

Her voice softens.

SAANVI
If I wanted to scare you,
you wouldn’t still be sitting here.

Silence returns.

But it’s different now.

Not empty.
Not loud.

Just… shared.

Fade out.
